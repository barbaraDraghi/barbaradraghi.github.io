# Barbara Draghi  
*Machine Learning Expert in Digital Health*

I am a Machine Learning Specialist at <a href="https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/medicines-and-healthcare-products-regulatory-agency" target="_blank">MHRA</a>/<a href="https://cprd.com/" target="_blank">CPRD</a> (UK's Medicines and Healthcare products Regulatory Agency / Clinical Practice Research Datalink), where I have spent the past five years developing synthetic data solutions for real-world healthcare data. I have co-led the development of the Emulate platform, a self-service synthetic data generation service, leading the design and delivery of its core synthetic data generation methodology.

Alongside this role, I also wear a researcher hat, pursuing a part-time PhD in machine learning focused on how synthetic data can reduce healthcare inequalities by mitigating existing bias and supporting more fair and inclusive clinical research.
